# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique the proposed change, not the person. Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience, and make space for questions and corrections.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, unwanted sexual content, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not welcome. Do not use issues or pull requests for spam or unrelated promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. Enforcement should consider context and the effect on participants. These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, and other project-managed spaces.

For routine conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through the public contact options on [arkiental's profile](https://github.com/arkiental). If no suitable private channel is listed, use GitHub's report-abuse tools instead of posting personal details publicly. Do not include private information in a public issue.
